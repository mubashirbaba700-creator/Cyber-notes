### New commands or ideas
No commands today. Ideas I learned:
- Cybersecurity means protecting computers, networks and data from attacks.
- Offensive security means testing a system the way an attacker would, but with permission, to find weaknesses.
- Defensive security means protecting systems, spotting attacks and responding to them.
- A penetration tester (pentester) is a person who is allowed to attack a system to find problems before real attackers do.
- Ethical hacking always needs permission. Without permission it is illegal.
